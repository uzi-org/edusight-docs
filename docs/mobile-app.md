# The EduSight mobile app

EduSight comes as a phone app for **teachers** and **parents**, on Android and iPhone. It is built for schools where the network is slow, expensive or missing for part of the day.

School administrators, content editors and platform staff keep using the web app, which has the setup and Studio tools.

## For teachers

- **Today.** The learners in your classes who need support, with the reason and what helps, plus how many are on track.
- **Learners.** Your classes, with each learner's profile: strengths, room to grow, subjects over time, activities and notes from home.
- **Capture.** Three ways to get information in:
  - **Scan a mark sheet.** Photograph a paper class list. EduSight reads the names and marks, and you check every value against the photo before anything is saved. Unclear values are highlighted.
  - **Enter marks** for one learner.
  - **Add an observation**: something you noticed today.

## For parents

- **My child** or **My children.** Each child's progress, strengths, room to grow and how to help at home. Children in different schools or classes are grouped by school and then class. Families never see risk levels.
- **Share a note from home.** Reading time, homework, focus and mood, or just a sentence.
- **Learn.** Short guides from the school and EduSight, in English and Kiswahili.

## Works offline

- Your classes and learners' profiles stay on the phone. You can open them with no connection, and the app shows when they were last updated.
- Marks, observations and sheet photos you capture offline are kept on the phone and sent automatically when the connection returns. Nothing is saved twice, even if the network drops halfway through sending.
- **Me → Waiting to send** lists anything not yet sent. If the school refuses something, for example a mark out of range, it stays there with the reason so you can fix or discard it.
- Reading a photographed sheet happens on the school's EduSight server, so the marks are ready to check once the photo has been sent.

## Privacy on the phone

- The app shows only what your account can see on the web.
- Your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage.
- Signing out removes cached learner information and anything not yet sent from the phone. The app warns you first if something has not been sent.
- Sheet photos are deleted from the phone once sent, and from the server once the marks are saved, as on the web.

## Languages

English and Kiswahili. The app follows your account's language, and you can change it under **Me**.
