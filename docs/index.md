# EduSight Africa

**Every learner should finish school, and finish it well.**

EduSight Africa helps schools notice early when a learner is starting to struggle, understand why, and act while there is still time to help. It works inside the tools a school already uses. A school management system, a learning platform or a ministry dashboard sends EduSight the information it already holds. EduSight returns a clear early-warning signal and practical next steps for the people who can help.

EduSight is part of [Uzi Org](https://github.com/uzi-org). *Uzi* is Kiswahili for *thread*. Uzi's work is to connect African communities with the knowledge and services that shape their lives, using technology that fits the resources they actually have. In education that means one thing above all: the world's best teaching and support should reach African learners, and no learner should fall out of school unnoticed.

## What EduSight does

1. **Listens to the signals schools already collect.** Attendance, assessment results, reading level, classroom engagement, and how involved the family is. No new data-entry burden is needed to get started.
2. **Raises an early-warning signal.** Each learner gets a support level: *routine*, *watch* or *urgent*. EduSight also explains in plain language what is driving it.
3. **Recommends the right kind of help.** EduSight names the kind of support that fits the learner: attendance follow-up, foundational literacy, academic catch-up, a wellbeing check-in, family partnership, or, where the pattern suggests it, a screening for learning needs by a qualified professional. Each recommendation names who should act and when to review progress.
4. **Keeps people in charge.** EduSight supports teachers, counsellors and school leaders. It does not replace them. Urgent cases and referrals are always marked for human review.

## Who it is for

| You are… | EduSight helps you… |
| --- | --- |
| **A teacher** | see which learners need attention this week, and what to try first |
| **A school leader** | direct limited support time, counsellors and catch-up classes to where they matter most |
| **A parent or guardian** | understand how to support your child at home, in supportive language, never as a label |
| **An edtech or school-management provider** | add early-warning and support guidance to your product through an API |
| **A district, ministry or NGO** | see where learners are at risk of leaving school, and whether interventions are working |

## How schools use it

- **As a platform.** Teachers and administrators sign in to EduSight directly. They see a support queue, learner profiles, intervention tracking and reports, on a phone or a laptop.
- **Inside your existing system.** Partners connect their school or student management system through the [integration API](integrate/index.md). EduSight keeps learner records in step, scores new information as it arrives, and notifies the partner system when a learner's support needs change.
