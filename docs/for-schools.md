# EduSight in your school

EduSight is built around a simple weekly rhythm: **notice, decide, act, review.**

## Who sees what

Every account sees only what its role needs. The same rules apply in the app and in the API.

| Role | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Teacher** | Learners in the classes assigned to them, with their signals, profiles and interventions |
| **School administrator** | Every learner in their own school; manages teachers' classes, guardian links and school reports |
| **Parent or guardian** | Only their own children, in any school using EduSight, grouped by school and then by class. Families see progress, strengths, growth areas and activities, never risk levels or probabilities |
| **Content editor** | No learner data; writes and publishes resources in EduSight Studio |
| **Platform administrator** | All schools, for support and oversight |

Staff accounts are created by school administrators. Anyone can sign up as a parent, but a parent sees nothing until the school links them to their child.

### Running your school in EduSight

School administrators have the tools to set the school up without technical help:

- **Accounts.** Create teacher and parent accounts, deactivate them, and choose which classes each teacher sees. A teacher sees only the classes you assign.
- **Learners.** Enrol learners one at a time, or import a whole class or school from a spreadsheet saved as CSV. Each row is checked, and problems are reported row by row.
- **Guardians.** Link a parent's account to their child from the child's page. Removing the link removes the parent's access immediately.
- **Access log.** Every time someone opens a learner's record, it is recorded. So is every change to accounts, classes and guardian links. The log cannot be edited. Families can see who has opened their own child's record.
- **Subjects.** Teachers and administrators keep the school's list of subjects, so assessments use consistent names.
- **Integrations.** Create and revoke API keys for your school management system.

Everyone has a **Settings** page for their name, language and password. Changing a password signs you out on every device.

## For teachers

- **The support queue.** Open the support queue to see the learners who need attention, most urgent first. Each one shows the main reasons and a suggested next step.
- **Learner profiles.** A learner's profile shows every subject against the learner's own history, their strengths and growth areas with suggested actions, their interests and activities, notes from families, and the interventions already tried.
- **Start an intervention in one step.** Turn a suggestion into a tracked intervention, such as a call to the guardian, a reading group or a catch-up plan. Set a review date.
- **Record what happened.** When you review, record whether the intervention helped. This record shows the school what works, and over time it helps EduSight give better suggestions.

## For school leaders

- See how many learners are at each support level, by class and over time.
- See which kinds of support are most needed. If many learners need foundational literacy support, for example, that may call for a school-wide reading programme rather than one-to-one help.
- Make sure every **urgent** learner and every **referral** has a named person responsible.
- Export reports for your board, district or funding partner.

## For parents and guardians

A parent with several children sees each one, grouped by school and then by class. Siblings at the same school but in different classes appear separately. A child at another school that uses EduSight appears under that school. For each child, families see how they are doing in every subject compared with their own earlier results, what they are good at and how to keep it up, where they need support and how to help at home, and their activities. Families can also record their child's interests.

The parent view also gives families a way to share what they see at home. That includes homework habits, reading, sleep and anything worrying the child. The school can then take it into account. Families never see risk scores or labels. A family view of practical suggestions that the teacher has approved is in development.

## Working on low-cost devices and weak connections

EduSight is designed to work on low-cost phones. It can be installed to the home screen like an app. It keeps working through short connection drops and shows a clear offline notice when there is no connection. See [Product status](status.md) for the offline features still in development.

## Resources and EduSight Studio

The resource library holds practical guides for teachers and families, filtered by category, level, language and tag. Families see only resources written for families.

Resources are written in **EduSight Studio**, a separate writing workspace for content editors and administrators. Writers use a visual editor with headings, lists, tip boxes, links and images; there is no markup to learn. Each resource has a summary, category, level, language, audience and tags, and stays a private draft until it is published.

## Languages

The interface is available in **English** and **Kiswahili**. More African languages are planned. We add a language only when native speakers have reviewed the translation.

## Getting started

1. Your school, or the platform you already use, connects to EduSight.
2. Learner records are synced from your existing school system. There is no need to retype them.
3. Teachers receive their first support queue once attendance or results are available.
4. In the first weeks, school leaders and teachers agree who handles urgent cases and referrals.
